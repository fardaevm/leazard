## San Francisco / California Bay Area

Verify against current law before relying on this.

- California caps most residential security deposits at one month's rent (AB 12, effective July 1, 2024), with an exception for some small landlords. A deposit equal to one month's rent is within the cap.
- Deposits must be returned within 21 days of move-out with an itemized statement (Civil Code 1950.5). A 21-day clause is compliant.
- Landlord entry generally requires 24 hours' written notice (Civil Code 1954). Such a clause is standard.
- Late fees have no fixed statutory cap but must be a reasonable estimate of landlord damages. A modest flat fee or a few percent of rent, after a grace period, is common.
- Month-to-month continuation after the initial term, smoking and vaping bans, pet consent requirements, occupancy limits, renter's insurance requirements, and governing-law clauses are common and lawful.
